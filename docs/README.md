# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

OctoAcme uses a consistent, lightweight, cross-functional project lifecycle. Named Project Managers and Product Managers provide delivery and outcome ownership, while developers, QA, stakeholders, and sponsors collaborate through clear artifacts and communication cadences.

## Project management overview

- **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define measurable success, identify stakeholders and roles, establish the initial timeline and risks, and decide whether work should enter planning.
- **Planning:** Hold a kickoff, prioritize a backlog with acceptance criteria, estimate scope, document the Definition of Done and test approach, and map dependencies and release milestones.
- **Execution and tracking:** Deliver small, reviewed increments through a project board, daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, demos, automated testing, and regular progress and blocker reporting.
- **Risk and communication management:** Maintain a visible risk register with owners and mitigations, communicate progress and decisions through a single source of truth, and escalate blockers through team, PM, product, and sponsor levels.
- **Release and deployment:** Verify acceptance criteria, CI, security scans, release notes, rollback plans, and smoke tests before deploying through a controlled pipeline and post-deployment verification.
- **Retrospectives and continuous improvement:** Capture successes and improvement opportunities after sprints, releases, and incidents, assign follow-up actions with owners and due dates, and review them regularly.
